- **Text fields: `TextEdit::word_motion(WordMotion::Unicode)` moves and deletes by word as AppKit does.** Option+Left/Right (Ctrl off macOS) and Option+Backspace/Delete stop at Unicode word boundaries, passing over punctuation: "one, two" stops after "one" and after "two" going forward, and "don't" and "a.b" stay one word. Chinese, Japanese and Thai split into dictionary words; a pill stays one word. The default, `WordMotion::Whitespace`, keeps stopping at white space, so existing fields move as before.
