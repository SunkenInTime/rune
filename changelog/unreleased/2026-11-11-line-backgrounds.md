- **Text: line-height backgrounds.** `Span::line_bg(color)` and `TextHighlight::line_bg()` fill each line a range's glyphs sit on from its top to its bottom, square and unpadded, as AppKit draws a `.backgroundColor` attribute: touching ranges and wrapped lines meet on whole device pixels without a gap or overlap, and the line's ending newline isn't filled. In a `text_input`'s `.spans` the fill follows edits and IME composition. Line fills paint under chips, rings and underlines. `Span::bg` and `TextHighlight` without it draw as before; `Span` literals still build (a line fill's `bg_pad` is `Span::LINE_BG`).
