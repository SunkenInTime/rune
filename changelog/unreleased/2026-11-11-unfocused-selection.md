- **Text fields: a selection that stays visible without the keyboard.** `SelectionStyle::unfocused(highlight, ink)` gives `El::selection_style` a third pair, for a field another element has the keyboard from (another field, a popover's field, a button): the field keeps drawing its selection in it, as AppKit draws a text view's selection in grey once it stops being first responder. Fields without it draw no selection while unfocused, as before.
