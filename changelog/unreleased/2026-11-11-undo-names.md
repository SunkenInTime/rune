- **Text fields: undo steps have names, for an Edit menu's "Undo Typing".** `TextEdit::undo_name()` and `redo_name()` return the name of the step Undo or Redo would act on: "Typing" for typing runs, line breaks, deletions and input method commits, as AppKit names them, "Paste" and "Cut" for those, and the app's own name for `TextEdit::undo_group_named(name, |e| ..)` (or `set_undo_name` inside a group). `can_undo()` and `can_redo()` tell whether there's a step at all. Rune's names come from `rune::set_labels` (`Label::Typing` is new). Undo and redo themselves are unchanged.
