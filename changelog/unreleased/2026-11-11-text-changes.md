- **Text fields: edits report what they changed.** `TextEdit::report_changes()` keeps a record that `take_changes()` reads: a `TextChange` per edit, the byte range of the old text replaced and the replacement's length, for typing, deletion, paste, cut, input method commits, `insert`, `replace_text`, and undo and redo (the exact inverse of what they take back). For ranges an app keeps in step with the text, such as comments. Off by default.
