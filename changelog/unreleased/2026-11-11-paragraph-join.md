- **Text: a long multi-paragraph text shapes and breaks only the paragraphs an edit touched.** A wrapped text of 2 KB or more (a document `text_input`, a long `text`) is laid out a paragraph at a time, each paragraph cached by its own text, spans and pills, and the paragraphs are joined into one `parley::Layout` (`Layout::join_part`, added to a vendored Parley 0.12 in `vendor/`), so carets, hit tests, selections, decorations and drawing work on it unchanged. Typing into a 10,500-word draft costs about a tenth of what it did, and a highlight that moves within one paragraph (a sentence focus) reshapes that paragraph alone. The joined layout matches the whole text's glyph for glyph; texts where shaping could cross a newline (right-to-left or bidi control characters, line breaks other than `\n`, a paragraph opening on a mark or another script, a neutral one after non-Latin text) keep shaping whole. `TextEdit::rich_display_specs` no longer copies the text or scans it for code lines when there are none.
