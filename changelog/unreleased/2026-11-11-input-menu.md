- **`El::input_menu` adds an app's items to a text field's own menu.** On a `text_input` or around one, `.input_menu(|s, offset| PopupMenu, |s, id, offset, tx| ..)` puts the app's items (titles, enabled, checked, separators, submenus) before Rune's spelling suggestions, Cut, Copy and Paste, with a separator between, built for the text offset under the click (or the caret's, from the Menu key) and picked with that offset. `on_secondary_press` still replaces the whole menu; fields without the hook show the menu they did. No field menu opens where there are no native pop-up menus (Linux, the web).
