- **File dialogs: `Tx::open_files` picks several files.** Its callback gets `Ok(paths)` (empty when cancelled), through rfd's `pick_files` on macOS and Windows and the XDG portal's `multiple` option on Linux; `FileDialogs::open_files` is the async form and `FileDialogs::with_files` scripts its answer. Headless runs show it in `--tree` with `"multiple": true` and take `{"type": "file-dialog", "paths": [...]}`; `Runtime::resolve_file_dialog_paths` and `file_dialog_multiple` drive it in tests. The new `ScriptEvent::FileDialogPaths` is the public form of a `"paths"` answer.
